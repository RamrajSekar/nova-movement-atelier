Before your first visit

Know what to expect.

A few practical details to help you plan your appointment.

What happens at my first appointment?

Your appointment starts with a discussion of your concerns, the activities affected, and your goals. Your physiotherapist will assess your movement and discuss appropriate next steps with you. What happens during the visit depends on your needs and assessment.

What should I bring and wear?

Have your booking confirmation handy. If you have a referral, relevant reports, or claim details, ask the clinic which documents to bring. Choose comfortable clothing that allows you to move; contact the clinic if you need guidance for your particular assessment.

Clinic-specific preparation instructions are being confirmed.

How much time should I allow?

The booking service currently lists an initial sports and musculoskeletal consultation as 40 minutes and an initial dizziness and balance assessment as 60 minutes. Check the duration of your selected appointment before confirming.

Do I need a referral or funding approval?

Requirements depend on your appointment and funding arrangements. For Medicare or WorkCover enquiries, confirm the required paperwork, eligibility, and any payment arrangements with the clinic before your visit.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Check your confirmation for available appointment-management options, or call 0482 044 525. Contact the clinic to confirm its cancellation policy.
